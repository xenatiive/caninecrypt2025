TERMS OF SERVICE

  • All commissions will ONLY be accepted through Paypal or Ko-fi and in USD.

  • I have the right to decline a commission for any reason.

  • At least 50% payment must be received before I begin. Progress photos before full payment will be watermarked.

  • References are REQUIRED — visual preferred, but detailed text is acceptable.

  • Commissions for commercial use are ×2 regular price — must be discussed beforehand.

  • I may publicly share commissions unless privacy is requested before payment.

  • I may have academic/professional obligations that affect response times.

  • I require clear and concise communication and instruction.

  • If I’m unable to complete the commission, the commissioner is entitled to either:

  • A) a partial refund — if progress has been made.

  • B) a full refund — if little or no progress has been made.
