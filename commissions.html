Standard Commission Info

The prices listed within the tables are the base price for all commissions and all prices are subject to a potential increase for detailed characters, prompts, or props.
Commissions featuring additional characters start at +50% of the base price listed in the tables. Alternate versions of the same commission (eg: one with and w/o clothes) start at an extra 25% of the base price -- always ask the artist for a quote!

Please ensure you have read over, understand, and agree to my T.O.S! Feel free to reach out to the artist if anything on this page is unclear, or there are commission ideas that do not fit these standard table molds.

TERMS OF SERVICE

  • All commissions will ONLY be accepted through either Paypal or Ko-fi and in USD.

  • I have the right to decline a commission for any reason.

  • At least 50% of the full payment must be received before I begin working on the commission. Until the full payment is received, any progress photos will be delivered to the commissioner with a watermark.

  • References are REQUIRED — visual references are preferred and clearer, but in-depth text can work as well.

  • Commissions for commercial purposes are x2 the regular price! Must be discussed prior to payment.

  • I may publicly share the commission work I do. Please specify prior to payment if you'd like to keep it private.

  • I have academic/professional obligations that may affect drawing/response speed.

  • I require clear and concise communication and instruction.

  • If I’m unable to complete the commission, you are entitled to:

  • A) a partial refund — if I’ve made progress

  • B) a full refund — if I haven’t started
