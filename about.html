as much as i tend to be a private and shy person by nature, i still do enjoy people and do my best to still be somewhat approachable and sociable :3c

i've dipped my toes into many things. apart from digital art, i also thoroughly enjoy listening to bad music, creative writing, fursuit making (on occasion -- check out my gallery for that), and probably a million other half-assed and mostly forgotten hobbies throughout the years. if you were to ever ask me if i've done x or y creative thing, i probably have. i'm still in school and working on obtaining an honours degree in psychology, and i'm also currently dedicated to learning german on the side ... drück mir die daumen :b
